    Plan the weeks after rice

    Rice farmers have a narrow window between harvest and the next transplant. Next Crop tests whether a winter crop fits — and whether the weather at its operation date cooperates.

    The problem

    A late rice harvest, a wet operation day or an unknown field can turn a good rotation into a missed rice season.

    What we built

    Pick one of four rice landscapes, replay the crop, compare rotations, then test the same plan against three NASA POWER seasons. A field-shift search finds the nearest rice-date window that clears both checks in all three seasons.

    NASA data

    NASA POWER daily corrected precipitation and minimum temperature; NASA Blue Marble imagery; a Copernicus Sentinel-2 context image.

    Method and limits

    Local crop calendars come from cited extension and research sources. The planner uses a three-day rain window around each operation, an illustrative 30 mm review threshold, and fixed crop durations and stages. Weather is a regional grid estimate, not a field sensor; check locally before planting.

    Try it in 30 seconds

    1. Start with Kaiping and winter potato in 2024/25.
    2. Select Try a wet harvest: the plan switches to 2023/24, the field jumps to 11 March 2024, and the three-day total is 39.34 mm.
    3. Switch the season to 2024/25: the corresponding operation falls to 0.59 mm.
    4. Open the three-season check and click 2023/24 to see the wet window.
    5. After the wet result, open Find a safer window: the nearest robust window moves the harvest one day earlier and clears all three seasons.
